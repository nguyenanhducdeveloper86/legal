Llama Lines: Latin Square Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026. Applies to Llama Lines: Latin Square for iPhone.

Short version

Llama Lines is a latin-square number puzzle: every row, every column and every chained group of cells holds each number from 1 to N exactly once. There is no account. The app shows ads, and ads are the only reason it uses an advertising identifier.

What the app does on your device

  • Grids from 4x4 to 7x7. A chain hops sideways, up and diagonally, so the regions are drawn leashes rather than square boxes.
  • Every level is generated on the phone with exactly one solution; a clue is dropped only while the solver can still prove the answer is unique.
  • Hints place or repair one digit from that solution, undo covers every move, and pencil notes stay in the round until you clear them.
  • No countdown timer: each level has a move budget shown as moves remaining. Progress, coins and settings are stored on the phone only.

What we collect

  • No account, no sign-in, no profile. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Advertising identifiers are used only through Google AdMob, as described under Ads below.
  • Purchases are verified by the App Store. We receive sales and proceeds reports from Apple in aggregate; we do not receive your name, address or payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with data brokers.

Ads

Ads are supplied by Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the game screen, an interstitial after a finished board and, only when you choose to watch one in exchange for a hint, a rewarded video. To select and measure those ads, Google may process a device advertising identifier, an IP-derived coarse location, device type, operating-system version and ad interaction events. We receive aggregate revenue reports, never personal information about you.

Consent comes first. Before any ad is requested, the app runs Google's User Messaging Platform consent flow. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland that form lets you choose whether personalised ads may be based on your identifier; declining still serves non-personalised ads. On iOS you are also asked once, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether the app may track you across apps. You can change that at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and read Google's practices at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Buying the ad-free upgrade stops ad requests being made by the app. It does not delete data Google has already collected.

Purchases

Payment for any purchase is handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your card, your billing address or your Apple Account credentials. Apple tells the app whether an entitlement is active; the receipt stays on your device and, for a subscription, with Apple. Apple's handling of your data is described at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Network use

The app uses the network only for:

  • requesting and displaying ads, and the consent form that precedes them;
  • checking an App Store purchase with Apple.

Everything else it does happens on the phone, without a connection.

Storage and deletion

Your data — the records, progress, history and settings this app keeps — is stored in the app's own container on the phone. It is not uploaded and there is no restore service. Deleting the app deletes it, and any file you exported beforehand stays wherever you sent it.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

We hold no personal data that identifies you, so there is nothing on our side to export, correct or delete. Data on your device is under your control: change it in the app, or delete the app. For the advertising and analytics data described above, Google is the controller of that processing and its privacy pages explain how to exercise your rights there. If you have a question about this policy or a request under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA, write to nguyenanhducdeveloper@gmail.com.
