WHAT WE COLLECT

Nothing.

We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics service, no crash-reporting service and no advertising identifier in these apps.

WHAT IS STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

Calculations, saved records, puzzle progress, settings and purchase state are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere. Deleting an app deletes this data. Some apps can export a PDF or a CSV file; those files are created on your device and go only where you choose to send them.

PURCHASES

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, your payment details or your Apple Account. Apple provides us with anonymous, aggregated sales reports that cannot identify you. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

ADS

Some of our games may show advertising supplied by Google AdMob. Where ads are shown, Google may use a device identifier and approximate information such as device type and coarse region to select and measure ads. We do not receive personal information from Google, only aggregate revenue reports.

You can limit ad tracking in iOS under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and you can review Google's practices at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Apps that currently show no advertising are listed above without ads. If advertising is added to an app later, this policy already covers it.

CHILDREN

These apps are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

YOUR RIGHTS

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on our side. Data on your device is under your control: delete a record in the app, or delete the app.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new date.
