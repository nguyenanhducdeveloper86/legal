catPet Support

Last updated 23 November 2026.

catPet is a small iPhone app for keeping a record of your cat: the weight you enter at each weigh-in, the points you record and the settings you choose. Everything is kept on the device, so most fixes happen on your phone rather than on a server.

Contact us

Email support@xcape.build. Include the app name, the app version (Settings → Apps → catPet, or the About screen), your iPhone model, the iOS version, and what you did just before the problem. We answer within two business days.

Common questions

I lost my data.

Everything is stored only on this iPhone. There is no account, no cloud backup and no restore service, so we cannot recover records for you. Deleting the app deletes its data, and reinstalling starts empty.

My cat is losing weight. What does the app say?

catPet only stores the weights and points you enter; it does not diagnose anything and does not give veterinary advice. A trend is arithmetic on your own entries. If a cat is losing weight, ask a veterinarian.

How do I turn the sound off?

Open the app's settings and turn sound off. The choice is kept on this device; there is no account to change it on.

Where is my data?

On your device only. There is no account and no cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes its data, and there is no backup to restore it from.
