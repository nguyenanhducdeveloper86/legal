Common questions

Nothing exports.

Check iOS Settings → Apps → ContactShelf → Contacts. Without read access the app has nothing to export. Full access is required; limited access shows only the contacts you selected.

Where does the exported file go?

Wherever you send it from the share sheet — Files, Mail, AirDrop, another app. ContactShelf does not keep a copy on a server because there is no server.

Is this a backup?

It is an export tool. Keep the exported file somewhere safe, and check an export before importing it into another address book.

Purchases and restoring

Purchases are handled by Apple. On a new phone or after reinstalling, open the purchase screen, tap Restore Purchases, and make sure the device is signed in with the same Apple Account that bought it. Purchases cannot be moved between Apple Accounts.

Cancel a subscription

iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal and keeps access until the paid period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel anything.

Refunds

We cannot issue refunds — Apple handles all billing. Request one at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my data?

On your device only. There is no account and no cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes its data. Export anything you want to keep first.
