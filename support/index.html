SUPPORT

These apps are made by an independent developer. If something is broken, or a number looks wrong, write to us — we answer.

Email: nguyenanhducdeveloper@gmail.com

Reply within two business days.

BEFORE YOU WRITE

Most questions are answered below.

PURCHASES AND RESTORING

Purchases are handled by Apple. If you changed phone or reinstalled:

1. Open the app.
2. Tap the price or the unlock button.
3. Choose "Restore purchase".

If the unlock does not come back, make sure the device is signed in with the same Apple Account that bought it. Purchases cannot be transferred between Apple Accounts.

REFUNDS

We cannot issue refunds — Apple handles all billing. Request one at https://reportaproblem.apple.com

DOES THE APP WORK OFFLINE

Yes. Every app in this collection works without a network connection. Nothing is uploaded; calculations, puzzles and records stay on your device.

WHERE IS MY DATA

On your device only. There is no account and no cloud sync. Deleting an app deletes its data, so export anything you need first.

HOW DO I EXPORT

Calculation apps: open the history, then share a record as a PDF.
Contact export: choose the fields, then share as CSV or vCard.

A CALCULATION LOOKS WRONG

Every calculation shows the formula and the table it came from. Check the result against the code edition adopted in your jurisdiction — local amendments can change requirements.

If you believe a value is wrong, email us with the app name, the inputs you used and the result shown. We treat those reports as bugs.

WHY DOES THE APP SAY IT IS NOT A SUBSTITUTE FOR A LICENSED PROFESSIONAL

Because it is not. The apps perform published formulas and table lookups; they do not replace a licensed electrician, nurse, engineer or driving instructor, and they do not replace your jurisdiction's code book.

PRIVACY

See the privacy policy. In short: nothing is collected.

ACCESSIBILITY

If a screen is hard to use with VoiceOver or large text, tell us which screen and which device — it is a bug we will fix.
