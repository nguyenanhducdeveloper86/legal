PermitPrep Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026. Applies to PermitPrep for iPhone.

Short version

PermitPrep is practice for the California learner's permit test: 150 hand-written questions, 40 per session, progress saved on the phone. There is no account. Nothing you type, open or save is uploaded.

What the app does on your device

  • Sessions draw 40 questions from 150, each with four answers, one correct choice and a short explanation.
  • Every answer is saved on the device, so a session resumes where you stopped.
  • Ten handbook topics, from right-of-way and signs to BAC limits, sharing the road and licence penalties.
  • Only the California set is available in this version; other states are marked as coming and are not sold as content.

What we collect

  • No account, no sign-in, no profile. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • No advertising identifier and no advertising SDK are used by this app.
  • Purchases are verified by the App Store. We receive sales and proceeds reports from Apple in aggregate; we do not receive your name, address or payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with data brokers.

Purchases

Payment for any purchase is handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your card, your billing address or your Apple Account credentials. Apple tells the app whether an entitlement is active; the receipt stays on your device and, for a subscription, with Apple. Apple's handling of your data is described at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Storage and deletion

Your data — the records, progress, history and settings this app keeps — is stored in the app's own container on the phone. It is not uploaded and there is no restore service. Deleting the app deletes it, and any file you exported beforehand stays wherever you sent it.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

We hold no personal data that identifies you, so there is nothing on our side to export, correct or delete. Data on your device is under your control: change it in the app, or delete the app. If you have a question about this policy or a request under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA, write to nguyenanhducdeveloper@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears at this address with a new date above. A change that matters will be noted in the app's release notes.
