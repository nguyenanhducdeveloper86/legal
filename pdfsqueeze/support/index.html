PDFSqueeze Support

Last updated 19 November 2026.

Contact us

Email nguyenanhducdeveloper@gmail.com. Include the app name, the app version (Settings → Apps → PDFSqueeze, or the About screen), your iPhone model, the iOS version, and what you did just before the problem. We answer within two business days.

Common questions

My text is no longer selectable.

Compression turns each page into an image — that is how the file gets smaller. Keep the original if you need a searchable document.

It says the target cannot be reached.

Very small targets on long documents can drop below readable quality. Raise the target, or split the document and compress the pages you need to shrink.

Is anything uploaded?

No. PDFSqueeze reads and writes only files you pick, and it works in airplane mode.

Purchases and restoring

Purchases are handled by Apple. On a new phone or after reinstalling, open the purchase screen, tap Restore Purchases, and make sure the device is signed in with the same Apple Account that bought it. Purchases cannot be moved between Apple Accounts.

Cancel a subscription

iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal and keeps access until the paid period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel anything.

Refunds

We cannot issue refunds — Apple handles all billing. Request one at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my data?

On your device only. There is no account and no cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes its data. Export anything you want to keep first.
