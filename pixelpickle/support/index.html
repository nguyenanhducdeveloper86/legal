Last updated 19 November 2026.

Contact us

Email nguyenanhducdeveloper@gmail.com. Include the app name, the app version (Settings → Apps → Pixel Pickle: Nonograms, or the About screen), your iPhone model, the iOS version, and what you did just before the problem. We answer within two business days.

Common questions

How do I remove the ads?

Buy Remove Ads in the shop, then Restore Purchases on another device signed in with the same Apple Account. It stops banner and interstitial requests for good; the optional reward video stays optional.

I am stuck on a puzzle.

You never have to guess: mark the cells you have proven empty with X notes and re-read the clues — a row or column with one long run plus an X or two usually collapses. A hint fills a single cell your own notes already prove, so it never reveals the picture for you.

Do I need a subscription?

No. There is no subscription in this app. Every purchase is charged once, and the free game plays every level in the trail.

Can I turn off ad tracking?

Yes. iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EEA and UK, Google's consent form appears before ads are requested the first time, and declining still leaves non-personalised ads.

Where is my progress?

On this phone only. There is no account and no cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes progress, coins and skins.

Purchases and restoring

Purchases are handled by Apple. On a new phone or after reinstalling, open the purchase screen, tap Restore Purchases, and make sure the device is signed in with the same Apple Account that bought it. Purchases cannot be moved between Apple Accounts.

Cancel a subscription

iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal and keeps access until the paid period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel anything.

Refunds

We cannot issue refunds — Apple handles all billing. Request one at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my data?

On your device only. There is no account and no cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes its data. Export anything you want to keep first.

Accessibility

If a screen is hard to use with VoiceOver, large text or a switch control, tell us which screen and which device — we treat it as a bug.
