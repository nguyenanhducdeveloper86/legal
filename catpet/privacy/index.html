catPet Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026. Applies to catPet for iPhone.

Short version

catPet keeps a record of your cat on your iPhone: the weight you enter at each weigh-in, the points you record and the settings you choose, all stored on the device. There is no account. Nothing you type, open or save is uploaded.

What the app does on your device

  • Weigh-ins you enter, with the date, so the change over time stays visible.
  • The points you record in the app.
  • The app's own settings, including whether sound is on.
  • All of it stays in the app's own storage on the phone, with no account and no sync.

What we collect

  • No account, no sign-in, no profile. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • No advertising identifier and no advertising SDK are used by this app.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with data brokers.

No server and no analytics. This version of catPet has no backend, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no in-app purchases. Nothing you enter or do is sent to us or to anyone else.

If a later version adds advertising or in-app purchases, this page will be updated before that version is released.

Storage and deletion

Your data — the records, progress, history and settings this app keeps — is stored in the app's own container on the phone. It is not uploaded and there is no restore service. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

We hold no personal data that identifies you, so there is nothing on our side to export, correct or delete. Data on your device is under your control: change it in the app, or delete the app. If you have a question about this policy or a request under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA, write to support@xcape.build.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears at this address with a new date above. A change that matters will be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Privacy questions: support@xcape.build. The publisher is an independent developer, and this address reaches the person who wrote the app.
